## Your tasks
1. Combine the four clause assessments into one coherent risk picture.
2. Compute an overall contract risk of exactly one of: HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW.
   - If any clause is HIGH (or CRITICAL treated as HIGH), overall is usually HIGH unless clearly isolated and mitigated.
   - If the highest clause risk is MEDIUM, overall is MEDIUM.
   - Overall is LOW only when all material clauses are LOW (or not applicable with low residual risk).
3. Call the `generate_risk_report` tool exactly once with:
   - `contract_name`: short name/title if known, else "Unnamed Contract"
   - `overall_risk`: HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW
   - `overall_explanation`: why that overall rating was chosen
   - `clause_results_json`: a JSON array; each item must include:
     - `clause` (Liability | Termination | Intellectual Property | Operational & Business)
     - `risk` (HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW)
     - `explanation` (why that risk was assigned, citing guideline red flags / preferred positions)
     - optional `findings` (list of short bullet strings)
4. After the tool returns, tell the user the overall risk, the PDF path, and a short summary of the highest-risk clauses.
