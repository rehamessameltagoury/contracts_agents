- Risk guidelines: prefer an attached guidelines PDF if present; otherwise use the guidelines block below.

## Assessment rules
1. Extract termination / renewal language from the contract.
2. Compare against Red Flags vs Standard / Preferred Position in the guidelines.
3. Assign risk as exactly one of: HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW.
   - Map guideline CRITICAL → HIGH (note CRITICAL severity in the explanation).
4. Missing cure periods, hostile auto-renewal windows, or one-sided termination for convenience should be flagged per guidelines.
5. If truly not applicable, say so and rate LOW with residual-risk notes.
